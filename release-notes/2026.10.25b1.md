Fix deprecated device-registry lookups for Nest Yale name and identity updates.

## Highlights
- Scope device lookups to the integration's config entry on Home Assistant 2026.8 and later, addressing the `async_get_device` deprecation scheduled for removal in Home Assistant 2027.8.
- Preserve device-ID and serial-number lookup behavior for name, firmware, and serial updates.
- Retain compatibility with older supported Home Assistant versions.

## Validation
- All 23 local unit tests passed, including config-entry isolation, serial fallback, and older-registry compatibility.
- Python compilation and `git diff --check` passed.
- Live Home Assistant validation is pending. This prerelease does not address the separate `aiohomekit` protocol error or LG TV availability error.

## HACS testing
1. Open the integration in HACS and enable **Show beta versions**.
2. Select **Redownload** and choose `2026.10.25b1`.
3. Restart Home Assistant.
4. Check that the Nest Yale `device_registry.async_get_device` deprecation warning no longer appears during startup or subsequent updates.
5. Confirm normal lock operation and that device names, firmware, serial numbers, and manually assigned areas remain correct.

The latest stable release remains `2026.10.07`. Stable publication and merging to main will follow successful testing.
